🔎 Key Insights for Smart City Energy Project

Certain zones show significantly higher total consumption, indicating possible commercial or industrial clusters driving demand.

Industrial consumers consistently rank among the top energy users, suggesting targeted efficiency programs could yield high impact savings.

Monthly consumption trends show increasing usage in specific periods, possibly due to seasonal cooling/heating demand.

Zones with high outage minutes also show high peak usage, indicating infrastructure stress during load spikes.

Faulty meters are concentrated in specific zones, suggesting maintenance prioritization is required.

Weekend peak usage is lower than weekday peak usage, implying commercial activity drives maximum load demand.

Low efficiency zones (high usage + outages) require grid modernization, load balancing, or smart monitoring deployment.
