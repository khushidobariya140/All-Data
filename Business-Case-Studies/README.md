# Business Case Studies

A repository dedicated to real-world business case studies, combining data analytics, SQL queries, Power BI reporting, and executive insight generation.

## Featured Case Study: WattWatch Urban Energy Analytics

### Overview
**WattWatch** analyzes urban energy consumption patterns for smarter city planning, resource allocation, and demand-side management.

### Key Insights & Findings
1. **Energy Demand Concentration:** Residential consumers account for the highest total energy consumption compared to commercial and industrial segments, highlighting the need for residential smart metering and demand-side management.
2. **Zone-Based Energy Hotspots:** Specific geographic zones drive major peak loads, requiring targeted grid infrastructure upgrades.
3. **Data Integration & Insights:** SQL analytics paired with Power BI visualization enabled key operational recommendations for city planners.

## Project Contents

- `WattWatcheddashboard.pbix`: Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- `SmartCity_SQL.sql`: Analytical SQL queries for energy consumption data.
- `SmartCityEnergy.csv` & `Date_Table.csv`: Datasets.
- `Key Insights.txt`: Executive summary of business recommendations.
